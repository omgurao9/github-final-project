# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

Community leaders may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

They are expected to respond to reported incidents promptly, consistently, and fairly while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

Examples of representing the community include using an official email address, participating through an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement.

Reports should include enough information to understand what happened, when it happened, where it happened, and who was involved.

Community leaders will review reports promptly and fairly. They may gather additional information from the people involved and will keep reports confidential when appropriate.

When a violation is confirmed, community leaders may take corrective action based on the severity and circumstances of the incident. Possible actions include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restriction from community participation, or permanent removal from the community.

Retaliation against anyone who reports an incident in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when determining the consequences for violations:

1. **Correction:** A private discussion or reminder when the behavior is inappropriate but does not require a stronger response.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning when inappropriate behavior continues or is more serious.
3. **Temporary Ban:** A temporary restriction from participation when there is a serious or repeated violation.
4. **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from community participation when there is a severe violation or continued unacceptable behavior.

Enforcement decisions should consider the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior.
